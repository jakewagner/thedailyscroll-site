We all know our phones are engineered to draw us in. To make us lose track of time while we scroll. To keep something playing in the background. To convince us to buy things we don’t need. To switch our brains off. Daily Scroll Club is for people who are tired of losing the day to a deluge of videos they cannot even remember.

Club members may experience:

  1. Increased sense of calm.
  2. More restful sleep.
  3. Greater presence with others.
  4. Boosted self-esteem.
  5. Rise in optimism.
  6. And a whole host of other things.

How it works:

  1. Choose

    Pick the apps and sites you want to block for most of the day.

  2. Access

    Access the blocked apps only during the scheduled hour.

  3. Together

    Start a club chapter to share the same schedule with your friends.

Catastrophe case:

The club is about being conscious of usage, not fully abstaining. Three emergency access sessions reset monthly. You never know when you might need to get into a blocked app for something.
